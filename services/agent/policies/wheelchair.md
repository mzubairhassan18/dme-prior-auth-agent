# Manual Wheelchair (K0001) Coverage

Synthetic Medicare Part B policy for standard manual wheelchairs. Created for the DME
prior-authorization agent demo.

## Coverage criteria

A standard manual wheelchair (HCPCS K0001) is covered when **all** of the following are
documented:

- The member has a mobility limitation that significantly impairs the ability to perform
  activities of daily living in the home, such as toileting, feeding, dressing, or moving
  between rooms.
- The member cannot safely use a cane, walker, or crutches, or the use of those devices is
  not functionally practical given the member's condition and home environment.
- A mobility examination by the treating practitioner documents strength, range of motion,
  balance, and the member's ability to transfer safely into and out of the wheelchair.
- A home assessment confirms that the member's home can accommodate the device and that
  the device is primarily for in-home use. Measurements of doorways, thresholds, and
  turning space are required when the home has known tight spaces.

## Documentation requirements

The prior-authorization packet must include:

1. Face-to-face mobility examination by the treating practitioner performed within the
   past 6 months.
2. Physician order specifying the base code K0001 and any required accessories (for
   example, elevating leg rests or anti-tip devices) with medical justification.
3. Home assessment documenting accessibility, including thresholds, door width, and ramps
   when applicable.
4. Statement of the member's primary mobility limitation and why a walker or cane is not
   sufficient.

Requests missing the home assessment, or requests that describe wheelchair use only for
community outings, are commonly denied.

## Quantity and replacement

- One manual wheelchair per member is covered.
- Replacement is covered every **5 years**, or sooner when the device is irreparably
  damaged or the member's condition changes such that the current device no longer meets
  their needs (documented by the treating practitioner).

## Exclusions and limitations

- Power wheelchairs require a separate prior authorization and are not covered under this
  policy.
- Wheelchairs intended for use primarily outside the home are not covered by this policy.
- Sport or recreational wheelchairs and upgrade options without documented medical
  necessity are excluded.
