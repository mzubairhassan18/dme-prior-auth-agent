# Rolling Walker (E0143) Medical Necessity

Synthetic coverage policy for four-wheeled rolling walkers with hand brakes. Used by the
DME prior-authorization agent demo.

## Coverage criteria

A rolling walker (HCPCS E0143) is covered when the member has a documented mobility
impairment and **all** of the following apply:

- The member cannot ambulate safely without an assistive device, demonstrated by gait
  instability, unsteadiness, or a history of falls. Two or more falls in the past 60 days
  is strong supporting evidence.
- A physical therapy or occupational therapy evaluation documents that a rolling walker is
  appropriate and that a cane or standard walker is insufficient for the member's needs.
- The member has sufficient upper-extremity strength and cognitive ability to operate the
  device safely, including the hand brakes.
- The device is intended for use in the home, or for community ambulation when the home
  assessment confirms the member's primary mobility limitation.

## Documentation requirements

The authorization request must include:

1. Treating practitioner order with the specific HCPCS code and quantity.
2. Physical or occupational therapy evaluation describing gait, balance, fall history,
   and why a rolling walker (rather than a cane or standard walker) is required.
3. Home assessment or practitioner statement that the device is medically necessary for
   activities of daily living.
4. For members in a facility, documentation that the device will be used for discharge to
   the community.

Incomplete therapy notes and requests that describe convenience rather than medical
necessity are common denial reasons.

## Quantity and replacement

- One rolling walker per member is covered.
- Replacement is covered every **5 years**, or sooner with documentation of irreparable
  damage, loss due to theft with a police report, or a significant change in medical
  condition (for example, weight change beyond the device's rated capacity).

## Exclusions and limitations

- Walkers used primarily for sports or recreation are excluded.
- Bariatric-rated walkers require documentation of weight exceeding the standard device's
  rated capacity; the standard E0143 is not upgradeable for convenience alone.
- Deluxe features (seat, basket, extra-wide frame) are not separately reimbursable unless
  medically necessary and documented.
