# Medicare CPAP Coverage Criteria

This document summarizes coverage for continuous positive airway pressure (CPAP) devices
and related supplies under the demonstration Medicare Part B benefit. It is synthetic
policy text created for the DME prior-authorization agent demo.

## Coverage criteria

Medicare covers a single CPAP device (HCPCS E0601) when **all** of the following are met:

- The patient has a diagnosis of obstructive sleep apnea (OSA, ICD-10 G47.33) confirmed by
  a sleep study. Coverage requires an apnea-hypopnea index (AHI) of **at least 15 events
  per hour**, or an AHI of **5 to 14 events per hour** with documented comorbidities such
  as hypertension, coronary artery disease, heart failure, stroke, or diabetes.
- A face-to-face clinical evaluation by the treating practitioner documents OSA symptoms,
  including excessive daytime sleepiness (Epworth Sleepiness Scale score of 10 or more is
  typical), impaired cognition, mood disorders, or documented hypertension.
- The patient is given a **three-month trial** of CPAP therapy. Continued coverage after
  the trial requires objective evidence of adherence: use of at least **4 hours per night
  on at least 70 percent of nights** during a consecutive 30-day period.
- A signed physician order and a copy of the sleep study report are on file.

## Documentation requirements

The prior-authorization packet must include:

1. Sleep study results with AHI, oxygen saturation nadir, and study type (in-lab
   polysomnography or home sleep apnea test).
2. Treating practitioner's face-to-face evaluation note describing symptoms and
   comorbidities.
3. Completed CPAP trial adherence download or device report when requesting continued
   coverage beyond the initial rental period.
4. Physician order specifying the device, mode, and pressure settings.

Missing sleep study results, missing adherence data, or an AHI below threshold without
qualifying comorbidities are the most common denial reasons.

## Replacement supplies

- CPAP masks (without headgear): one per 3 months.
- Mask headgear: one per 6 months.
- Tubing: one per 3 months.
- Filters: two per month when the manufacturer specifies disposable filters.
- Humidifier chamber: one per 6 months.

Replacement requires documentation that the current item is worn, damaged, or no longer
functional — not merely that the replacement interval has elapsed.

## Exclusions and limitations

- CPAP is not covered for mild OSA (AHI below 5) without a qualifying syndrome.
- Devices used solely for snoring without a documented OSA diagnosis are excluded.
- A second CPAP device is not covered while the first device is functional.
- Auto-titrating devices require the same coverage criteria as fixed-pressure devices.
